02 → Principles

What I stand on.

  1. Design gets brought in last — and blamed for not saving it A co-author, not a contractor. Design enters the task before the brief is written. Otherwise it fixes symptoms, not the problem.
  2. Hired for the picture, judged on the meaning “Just design it” — the start of a weak project. Form doesn’t save a weak idea.
  3. Creative work is what survives others’ decisions A designer almost never works freely — they’re always negotiating with constraints.
  4. A couple of mockups never fixed a broken process Chaos rarely looks like chaos. More often, it looks like a temporary fix that quietly stayed forever.
Work is done
when it works
in reality.
03 → Evaluation

How I evaluate the work.

Four levels. A solution only passes
if it works on all four.

Level 1
Logic
The user gets it: where to tap, what happens, why it matters. If it needs explaining, the solution is weak.
Got it?
Level 2
Form
Hierarchy, contrast, rhythm, typography, balance. This is where craft lives — and where expression earns its place.
Crafted?
Level 3
System
Will it scale to ten channels? Hold up under new scenarios? Stay aligned with the brand?
Holds?
Level 4
Business
Does it help the business? Conversion, retention, recognition, speed.
Worked?

07 → Craft

Not a science. Not an art.

What people feel — and cannot put into words.

01 One brand red.

From the outsideOne brand red.

From the inside

Thousands of shades of one red. Warm reads as trust. Cool reads as alarm. Light disappears, dark crushes. Without context and a pair beside it, colour means nothing.

02 One button on the screen.

From the outsideOne button on the screen.

From the inside

Nine states. Eight aren’t in the screenshot. A button lives in motion — not in the mockup. A good one answers. A bad one doesn’t.

03 One typeface.

From the outsideOne typeface.

From the inside

Eighteen weights — for paragraphs, headings, numbers, quotes. Size raises the voice. Typography has its own laws. Reading is ours alone.

04 One composition on the page.

From the outsideOne composition on the page.

From the inside

Grid. Hierarchy. Rhythm. The air between blocks — also an element. What the eye reads before the word, and reacts to before the mind.

05 One logo.

From the outsideOne logo.

From the inside

A brand isn’t a logo. It’s repeated decisions: how we sound, how we look, how we move, how we hold the bar.

06 The task of drawing a mockup.

From the outsideThe task of drawing a mockup.

From the inside

Pulling colour, button, type and rhythm — into one feeling. So a person looks and wants, without knowing why. And couldn’t describe it if they tried.

Everyone talks about design.
No one really knows what it is.

Strong design
holds up in reality.
