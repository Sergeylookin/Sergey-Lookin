A Web3 universe of NFT characters and the first-ever NTO — from idea to launch.

Chobies was one of my most complex projects in systemic design. For the Choise ecosystem I built a generative character system for the first NTO (NFT Token Offering) format — combining NFTs and a tokenized economy.

The task wasn't to draw a set of pictures, but to build a full digital product — with a coherent identity and a clear user path from entry to purchase.

The system held over 500 parameters and assembled 1014 unique characters from dozens of independent traits. Beyond the generative engine I built the site with a minting dashboard, animated videos, dynamic banners and social content.

The project drew a sizable audience and brought revenue to both the brand and NFT holders. For me, it was a powerful school in building a digital product from scratch.

  • Generative system: dozens of independent traits — faces, eyes, outfits, accessories, backgrounds and colours.
  • A site and a minting & asset-tracking dashboard.
  • Animated videos and ad campaigns in one visual language.
  • The first NTO format: NFT plus a tokenized economy.
