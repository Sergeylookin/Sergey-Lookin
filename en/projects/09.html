A smart device that combines climate control with Bitcoin mining.

Heatbit is one of the world's first home heaters that also mine Bitcoin. I owned the product's visual packaging and worked closely with the client's team.

The main challenge was to explain a complex hybrid product so a person grasps its real value within seconds — and to package hard technology into an appealing consumer brand.

I shaped the visual identity, built a landing with a clear structure and product photography, and developed the product's visual language — without technical overload.

After launch, landing conversion reached 30–35%, the product became clearer, and the client moved from tests to steady sales through the site.

  • Worked directly with the client's team.
