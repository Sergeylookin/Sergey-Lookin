Hi, I’m Sergey Lukin

What people expect here: “I solve business problems,” “I make design that sells.” To sound weightier, you could add “I think strategically.” It’s all true, too — but everyone writes this, which means no one does. From here on, no slogans — only what’s visible from the inside.

Visuals don’t “sell.” Visuals spark desire, earn trust, stay in memory — everything that happens to a person in the first second, before meaning. If it isn’t the visual that makes the first impression, then what does?

I’ve worked where design is made by the ton, and where it’s tuned to the millimetre. The assembly line taught me speed — and that a weak decision, multiplied across a million touchpoints, becomes weak a million times over. The millimetres taught me that any compromise shows more than anything else.

My job is to turn an idea into an image that is at once precise, alive, emotional and clear. Each on its own — not hard. All at once — almost impossible. For twelve years I’ve been bringing them together.

The best solutions usually look as if no other option ever existed.
